## Course Syllabus Overview

* **Unit I: Foundations of Unsupervised Learning** 
  * **Topics:** Learning paradigms (Supervised vs. Unsupervised vs. Semi-supervised), problem formulation, exploratory data analysis, pattern discovery, similarity & distance metrics (Euclidean, Manhattan, Cosine), real-world use cases .
  * **Applications:** Customer segmentation, anomaly & fraud detection, recommendation systems, social network analysis, bioinformatics, document clustering .

* **Unit II: Partition-Based Clustering** 
  * **Topics:** Clustering fundamentals, hard vs. soft clustering, K-Means, K-Means++, K-Medoids (PAM), MiniBatch K-Means, data standardization, cluster validation (WCSS, Silhouette Score, Davies-Bouldin Index, Elbow Method) .
  * **Applications:** Retail analytics, image segmentation, telecom user profiling, hospital patient grouping, marketing campaign optimization .

* **Unit III: Hierarchical & Density-Based Clustering** 
  * **Topics:** Agglomerative & Divisive Clustering, linkage methods, dendrogram interpretation, DBSCAN, $\epsilon$-neighborhood, MinPts, noise handling, comparison of clustering algorithms .
  * **Applications:** Geographic hotspot detection, crime mapping, earthquake analysis, biological taxonomy, network intrusion detection .

* **Unit IV: Dimensionality Reduction & Representation Learning** 
  * **Topics:** Curse of dimensionality, Principal Component Analysis (PCA), manifold learning, t-SNE, UMAP, autoencoder intuition, feature representation .
  * **Applications:** Data visualization, face recognition, handwritten digit analysis, feature extraction, image compression .

* **Unit V: Association Rule Mining & Anomaly Detection** 
  * **Topics:** Support, Confidence, Lift, Conviction, Apriori, FP-Growth, Market Basket Analysis, Isolation Forest, Local Outlier Factor (LOF), novelty detection .
  * **Applications:** Product recommendation, fraud detection, cybersecurity, credit card anomaly detection, predictive maintenance .

* **Unit VI: Evaluation & Applications of Unsupervised Learning** 
  * **Topics:** Internal & external clustering validation metrics, Silhouette Score, stability analysis, interpretability, ethical AI, visualization dashboards, real-world case studies .
  * **Applications:** End-to-end customer analytics dashboard, AI-based business intelligence, healthcare analytics, smart city applications .
